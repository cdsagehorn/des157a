The worst space mission ever

Captain Alex had been chosen to lead the most crazy space mission in history. Everything was going according to plan until a giant giraffe suddenly appeared outside the spaceship.

The crew only had 5 minutes to figure out what to do. In a panic, someone grabbed a container of pizza and launched it out of the airlock.

While everyone was dancing, the spaceship suddenly changed direction and started flying toward Mars.

Without warning, every light turned purple, and aliens started flying around the cabin. Captain Alex looked at the crew and shouted, "Yikes!"

Somehow, the crew survived the mission. Nobody could explain what happened, but they all agreed on one thing: they were never going to space again!
