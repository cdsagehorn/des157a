A collection of ordinary things that carry memories, responsibilities, and pieces of who I am.

Move over a photograph to bring a memory into focus. Click it to discover its story.

These objects look ordinary until you remember why you kept them.

About this experiment

I am exploring the basic positioning and overlays approach using five of my own photographs. I layered the images like scattered memories and used grayscale filters and hover interactions to bring each object into focus. Clicking a photograph opens an overlay with the memory or responsibility connected to it.

By combining overlapping photographs, changing image filters, and story overlays, I wanted to explore how interaction can make ordinary objects more meaningful. For my next version, I want to experiment with selecting objects directly inside the backpack photograph to reveal their stories.
